Why Choose Porcelain for Your Leicester Patio

Frost-proof and weatherproof: Porcelain is fired at extremely high temperatures, making it non-porous and completely impervious to water. It won't crack in frost, stain from leaves or algae, or degrade in the persistent wet of a Midlands winter.

Zero maintenance: Unlike sandstone which requires annual sealing, or timber which needs staining and oiling, porcelain needs nothing except an occasional wash. It looks the same in year 20 as it did on installation day.

Stain-resistant: Red wine, oil, rust, moss — porcelain resists all of them. For families with children and pets, this is a significant practical advantage.

Large-format slabs: Porcelain is available in large formats — 600×900mm, 900×900mm and 1200×600mm — that create a clean, uncluttered surface with fewer joints. This makes a patio look bigger and more contemporary than smaller natural stone formats.

Colour consistency: Unlike natural stone where every slab is different, porcelain offers consistent colour across the whole patio — important for homeowners who want a precise, controlled aesthetic.

Porcelain Patio Installation — What Sets Us Apart

The most important thing about a porcelain patio isn't the slab — it's what goes underneath it. Leicestershire's clay soil is notorious for movement, and porcelain laid on anything other than a properly compacted sub-base will crack, rock or sink within 2–3 years. We excavate to a minimum of 150mm, lay a compacted MOT Type 1 sub-base, and use a sand/cement bed with flexible jointing compound rated for UK freeze-thaw cycles on every installation.

We've relaid porcelain patios that other contractors installed incorrectly — it's one of the most common jobs we're called in to remedy. Getting the sub-base right the first time is not optional.
