Fencing Styles We Install in Leicester

Closeboard (Feather Edge) Fencing

The most popular Leicester garden fence style — vertical featheredge boards fixed to horizontal arris rails between concrete posts. Strong, private, traditional in appearance and the best long-term value. Heights from 4ft to 6ft, with optional 1ft or 2ft trellis tops.

Lap Panel Fencing

Pre-made overlap panels are the quickest fence to install — ideal for replacing storm-damaged sections or fencing a long boundary on budget. Available in standard 6x6 and 6x5 sizes, fitted between concrete posts with gravel boards to prevent rot at ground level.

Slatted & Hit-and-Miss Fencing

Contemporary horizontal slats or alternating hit-and-miss boards give a modern designer look while still providing privacy. Often paired with porcelain patios and composite decking in newer Leicester developments. Both styles allow wind through, reducing storm damage.

Picket & Decorative Fencing

Low picket fences for front gardens, decorative trellis screens to divide garden zones, and bespoke timber screens with cut-out patterns. Painted or stained to your colour preference.

Concrete Post & Gravel Board Replacement

Replacing wooden posts and rotten gravel boards with concrete dramatically extends the life of any fence. We can replace just the posts and boards while keeping your existing panels, saving significant cost on full replacement.

Why Concrete Posts Matter — Especially in Leicester

Most fence failures in Leicester gardens are not panel failures — they are post failures. Timber posts rot at ground level within 8 to 12 years in our wet Midlands clay, and storm damage typically snaps timber posts long before the panels themselves fail. We use concrete slotted posts on every installation as standard, paired with concrete gravel boards that lift the timber clear of wet ground.

The result is a fence that survives every storm we have had in the last decade — including Storm Eunice in 2022 — and outlasts the timber panels themselves, which can be replaced individually without disturbing the posts. We have customers in Oadby, Wigston and Kirby Muxloe whose concrete posts from 15+ years ago are still going strong with only their second set of panels.

Every post is set in postcrete to a depth of 600mm minimum, fully levelled and plumbed, with full curing time before panels go in. No shortcuts.

Our Fencing Installation Process

Step 1 — Free Site Survey & Measurement

We visit your property in Leicester, measure the boundary accurately, discuss your preferred style and height, and check for any complications (existing concrete, tree roots, neighbour issues). Quote provided within 24 hours.

Step 2 — Material Ordering

Once approved, we order all materials direct from our trade suppliers. Most projects start within 7 to 14 days of quote acceptance.

Step 3 — Old Fence Removal

We dismantle the existing fence, remove old concrete footings where possible, and clear the line ready for new posts. All waste removed same day.

Step 4 — Post Setting

Concrete posts dug in to 600mm minimum, levelled in postcrete, left to cure overnight (or longer in cold weather).

Step 5 — Panel Fitting & Finishing

Gravel boards slotted in, panels fitted, cappings added, site swept and photographed. Most domestic fence runs are completed in 1 to 2 days.
