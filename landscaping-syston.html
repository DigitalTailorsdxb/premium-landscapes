Planning Permission & Syston

Local Planning Authority: Charnwood Borough Council

Responsible for planning decisions in Syston and the surrounding LE7 area.

Charnwood Borough Council is the local planning authority. Syston's historic town centre streets contain some listed buildings — work on or adjacent to these requires listed building consent. Standard domestic landscaping on non-listed properties is generally permitted development. Syston falls outside the National Forest but within Charnwood's landscape character areas, which may be a material consideration for larger external works.

What typically does not need planning permission in Syston

  • Rear garden patio and hard landscaping at ground level
  • Artificial grass in rear or front gardens (replacing natural grass)
  • Composite or timber decking up to 30cm above ground level and not more than 50% of the rear garden
  • Garden lighting using low-voltage (12V) transformers
  • Rear garden fencing up to 2 metres high

What may require planning consent in Syston

  • Decking over 30cm high or covering more than 50% of the rear garden
  • Front garden hard-surfacing over 5m² replacing grass (must use permeable surface or obtain permission)
  • Work in a designated conservation area or on a listed building
  • Large outbuildings or pergolas exceeding the permitted development size limits

Planning rules can change and are subject to local variations. We provide planning guidance at no charge during our survey visit — if in doubt, always verify with Charnwood Borough Council directly before proceeding.
