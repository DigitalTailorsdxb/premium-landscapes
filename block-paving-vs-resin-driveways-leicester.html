Block Paving vs Resin Driveways: Which Is Better for Leicester Homes?

By Lewis Reid · 6 years landscaping experience | 30 June 2026 | Driveway Guide

Block paving and resin bound gravel are the two most popular driveway surfaces in Leicester right now — and they come up in almost every driveway quote we do. Both are good products. The right one depends on your property type, your maintenance appetite, and what the ground underneath looks like.

This is an honest comparison from a contractor who installs both every week.

Cost Comparison

Block paving

£70–£110/m²

Standard residential block, herringbone or stretcher bond pattern

Resin bound gravel

£85–£130/m²

6mm aggregate, 18mm depth, standard colours

On a typical Leicester driveway of 40–50m², block paving runs £3,000–£5,500 and resin runs £3,500–£6,500. The gap narrows on smaller driveways where resin's faster application time offsets its higher material cost.

Appearance

Block paving suits traditional Leicester housing stock — 1930s semis, Victorian terraces, red-brick new builds — particularly well. Brick-style blocks in charcoal, brindle, or buff look like they were always there. The jointed appearance gives the driveway character and fits with the material palette of the house.

Resin bound has a smoother, more contemporary appearance — closer to a tarmac finish but with visible aggregate texture. It works better on modern homes, new builds, and properties where a cleaner look is the goal. Colour options are wide — from natural pea gravel tones to more unusual charcoal or golden hues.

One difference worth noting: block paving ages well and looks better with some patina. Resin can look tired after 10–15 years and is harder to repair invisibly when it does.

Maintenance

Block paving

  • Weed growth in joints if jointing sand washes out — preventable with polymeric jointing compound, which we use as standard
  • Moss and algae on north-facing driveways in Leicester's wetter months — treat with a biocide annually
  • Occasional lifting and relaying if individual blocks sink or crack — blocks are replaceable individually, which is a genuine advantage

Resin bound

  • No joint weeds — it's a continuous surface
  • Pressure wash annually to keep it looking clean
  • UV stability varies — cheaper resins yellow over time; we use UV-stable resin as standard
  • Repairs are more visible — patching resin rarely matches the original colour exactly after a few years

SUDS Compliance in Leicester

Since 2008, any front garden driveway over 5m² that's impermeable requires planning permission unless it drains to soft ground rather than the highway. Both block paving and resin can be installed permeably:

Permeable block paving uses open-jointed blocks on a free-draining sub-base — water passes between the joints. Fully SUDS compliant, no planning required.

Resin bound gravel is inherently permeable — water drains through the resin matrix into the sub-base below. No planning required regardless of size.

Both are legitimate compliant options. We design all Leicester driveways for SUDS compliance as standard — your quote will confirm the approach.

Access to Services Underneath

This is the practical consideration that doesn't come up enough: Leicester's older housing stock has drains, service pipes, and stopcock covers that sometimes aren't accurately mapped. If you need to access anything under your driveway:

Block paving: Blocks lift cleanly, the area is accessed, blocks relay back. Cost: half a day's labour, maybe £150–£300.

Resin: Has to be cut with an angle grinder, broken out, the work done, then patched. The patch will be visible. Cost varies but it's disruptive and not invisible.

For Victorian and Edwardian Leicester terraces with older drainage — LE1, LE2, LE3, LE4 — we lean towards recommending block paving for this reason alone.

Which Is Better for Leicester Clay Soil?

Both work on Leicester's clay soil with the right sub-base. The key for both is minimum 150mm MOT Type 1 hardcore compacted in layers, edging restraints to prevent lateral spread, and adequate drainage gradient away from the house.

Resin is slightly more forgiving of minor sub-base movement because it's a flexible matrix — small ground movement doesn't crack it the way it would crack a rigid surface. Block paving handles movement differently: individual blocks shift slightly but rarely crack, and can be relevelled.

Our Recommendation

Choose block paving if:

  • You have a traditional Leicester property
  • You want a proven 20+ year lifespan
  • You want flexibility to access services underneath
  • You're in an area where resin's contemporary look would be out of character

Choose resin if:

  • You have a modern or new-build property
  • You want a weed-free, truly low-maintenance surface
  • You want a smooth, contemporary finish
  • You're laying a smaller driveway where faster installation keeps costs down

Frequently Asked Questions

Is block paving or resin bound cheaper in Leicester?

Block paving is typically £10–£20/m² cheaper installed. On a 45m² driveway that's a £450–£900 difference. Resin's lower maintenance cost over time partially offsets this, particularly on larger driveways where weed control in block paving joints is more significant.

Which driveway surface lasts longer?

Both last 15–25 years with proper installation and maintenance. Block paving often outlasts resin because individual blocks can be replaced without affecting the rest. Resin surfaces are harder to repair invisibly as they age.

Can resin bound driveways crack?

Quality resin bound on a properly prepared sub-base is flexible and resists cracking. Failures typically occur from sub-base movement, tree root pressure, or UV degradation on low-quality resin products. We use UV-stable resin and always install on a compacted MOT Type 1 sub-base.

Do I need planning permission for a new driveway in Leicester?

Not if the surface is permeable (permeable block paving or resin bound gravel). Both comply with SUDS regulations and don't require planning permission regardless of size. We design all driveways for compliance.

How long does a driveway installation take in Leicester?

A standard 40–50m² driveway takes 2–3 days for block paving and 1–2 days for resin bound. Resin is faster to apply but requires longer curing time (24 hours) before use. Block paving can usually be driven on the same day laying is complete.
