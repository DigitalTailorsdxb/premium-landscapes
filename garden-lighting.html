Why Garden Lighting Transforms Leicester Gardens

Leicester's latitude means we lose outdoor daylight from around 4pm in midwinter. Well-designed garden lighting extends your enjoyment of the garden by months each year — turning an unusable dark space into an inviting outdoor room you can use from the evening meal through to late autumn.

Beyond lifestyle, garden lighting has a measurable impact on security and property value. A well-lit garden can make your property more visible and welcoming at night, and buyers consistently rate quality outdoor lighting as one of the features that make a property feel well-maintained and cared-for.

We design and install low-voltage LED garden lighting systems across Leicester and Leicestershire — from subtle path lighting and uplighting on specimen trees to fully zoned smart systems controlled from your phone. Every installation is designed with a proper electrical specification before a cable is laid.

What to Expect on Garden Lighting Installation Day

1

Design Walk-Through

We start with a site walk to confirm fixture positions, cable routes and transformer location. If combining with other landscaping, lighting cables are always laid before surfaces go down.

2

Cable Laying

Low-voltage cable is laid in conduit at the correct burial depth, routed to avoid plant root zones where possible. All cable joins are fully weatherproof.

3

Fixture Installation

Fittings are installed and connected. We check IP ratings for each position — anything near water features needs IP68 rated fittings.

4

Transformer & Control Setup

The transformer is installed, timer and dusk-to-dawn sensor configured, and smart controls set up if included.

5

Testing & Handover

Every fitting is tested, beam angles adjusted, and we walk you through the control system with a simple zone guide.

Common Garden Lighting Mistakes to Avoid

Wrong IP rating for the location

IP44 fittings are not suitable near ponds or anywhere subject to water submersion. Always check the IP rating matches the installation environment — we specify this correctly for every fixture.

Choosing the wrong colour temperature

Warm white (2700–3000K) creates an inviting atmosphere. Cool white (5000K+) looks clinical and harsh outdoors. Most Leicester garden projects use warm white throughout.

No circuit zoning

A single on/off switch for the whole garden is a missed opportunity. Zoning the system lets you set the right mood for different uses and reduces electricity costs.

Surface-mounting cables rather than burying

Cable clipped along fences or over surfaces is an eyesore and a trip hazard. All permanent garden lighting cables should be correctly buried or housed in conduit.

Patio and Pergola Lighting in Leicester

A patio or pergola is used most on warm evenings — so lighting isn't an optional extra, it's what makes the space functional after sunset. We install IP65-rated LED spotlights on pergola uprights and beams, recessed low-level lights into patio walls and steps, and colour-temperature adjustable strips that let you switch between warm white (relaxed evening mood) and cool white (working or cooking). All lighting is installed on a dedicated low-voltage (12V or 24V) transformer with a weatherproof outdoor controller so you can dim, switch zones and set timers without going inside.

See our pergola installation page for examples of combined pergola and lighting projects.

Decking Lights and Step Lighting

Recessed decking lights and riser lights are the most practical form of garden lighting for composite or timber decking: they mark the edge of each step clearly, eliminate trip hazards at night and require no additional cabling run across the surface. We plan and install all decking lights as part of the decking project so the cable is concealed in the subframe before the boards go down. Fascia-mounted lights are also popular — fitted into the horizontal board below each deck level, creating a strip of warm light that outlines the deck without glare.

See our composite decking page for combined decking and lighting projects.

Smart Garden Lighting Systems

We install smart garden lighting systems compatible with the major platforms — including systems that can be controlled via a smartphone app, set to respond to dusk/dawn sensors or programmed with weekly schedules. Smart controllers can manage multiple lighting zones independently, so your patio, pathway, driveway and pergola lighting can all be on different schedules and brightness levels. All smart systems we install use low-voltage transformers and are suitable for permanent outdoor installation across Leicestershire's climate.

Garden Lighting as Part of a Full Garden Makeover

The most cost-effective time to install garden lighting is during a full garden makeover, before paving, decking or rendered walls are finished. Cable can be laid under hard surfaces before they go down and conduit can be built into walls during construction — a significant saving compared to retrofitting lighting into a finished garden later. If you're planning a full garden transformation, we plan the lighting scheme at the design stage and install it as a single coordinated project.
