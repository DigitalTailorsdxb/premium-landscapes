---
name: Company-first positioning
description: Lead with landscaping quality and personal service, not instant AI tools.
---

Present Premium Landscapes as an experienced landscaping company first: 15 years’ experience, quality workmanship, pride in every detail, close customer collaboration and gardens planned around a workable budget. Keep real completed projects prominent. Instant designs and quotes are supporting options, not the main promise.

**Why:** The owner explicitly wants the website to sell the company and its service rather than focus on instant designs and quotes.

**How to apply:** Use this positioning for page introductions, calls to action and SEO copy. Prioritize WhatsApp and calls; keep the online quote tool available without making it the primary selling point. Do not invent awards, guarantees or customer outcomes to support the message.

Use genuine completed-project photos for service imagery wherever available, and choose crops that clearly show the relevant work.

**Why:** The owner finds the product images too AI-looking and specifically flagged a patio photo dominated by a fence.

**How to apply:** Prefer authentic project photography over generated garden scenes. Preserve natural colours, avoid strong brightness filters, and do not imply a concept image is completed work.
