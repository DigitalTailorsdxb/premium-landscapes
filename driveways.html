Our Process

A clear, transparent process from first contact to project handover.

2

Site Survey

We assess the size, existing drainage, crossover condition and edging requirements. We'll advise on the best material for your specific property.

3

Excavation & Sub-Base

Existing surface and topsoil are removed. A compacted MOT Type 1 sub-base of 100–150mm is installed — the foundation of a long-lasting driveway.

6

Finishing & Clean-Up

Jointing compound or kiln-dried sand is brushed in, the surface is compacted and sealed if required. Site fully cleared before handover.

Block Paving Driveways in Leicester

Block paving remains the most popular driveway surface across Leicester and Leicestershire — chosen for its durability, its repairability and the huge range of colours, shapes and laying patterns available. From classic herringbone and basket weave to stretcher bond and random pattern, we help you choose a design that complements your home's character. All block paving driveways are installed with a concrete haunch edging, compacted MOT Type 1 sub-base and kiln-dried sand jointing for a finish that lasts 20+ years and can be individually repaired if a slab is ever damaged.

What to Expect on Driveway Installation Day

1

Excavation & Removal

Existing surfaces are broken up and removed from site. We excavate to the correct depth — typically 200–300mm depending on surface type and vehicle loading.

2

Sub-Base Laying

A compacted MOT Type 1 sub-base is laid and mechanically compacted. This is the most critical stage — a driveway is only as good as what's underneath it.

3

Edging & Channels

Edging restraints are set, and drainage channels are installed where required to manage surface water correctly.

4

Surface Installation

The chosen surface is installed to the manufacturer specification. Block paving is individually laid and compacted; resin bound is trowel-applied in a single continuous pour.

5

Finishing & Clean-Up

Joints are filled or sealed, the surface is cleaned down, and all waste is removed. Most driveways can be used by foot within hours; vehicles after 24–48 hours.

Common Driveway Mistakes to Avoid

Ignoring drainage requirements

Since 2008, front driveways in England over 5m² must use a permeable surface or drain to a soakaway. Installing an impermeable surface without proper drainage is technically a planning breach.

Choosing the cheapest contractor

Driveway failures — cracking, lifting, sinking — are almost always sub-base failures. A lower quote often means a thinner, uncompacted base that guarantees expensive remedial work.

No edging restraints

Block paving without proper edging will spread and loosen over time, especially on gentle slopes. Concrete haunching or proprietary edge restraints are essential on every installation.

Wrong resin thickness

Resin bound surfaces laid too thin (under 18mm) are brittle and prone to cracking, especially where vehicles turn. We always lay to the manufacturer's minimum recommended depth.
