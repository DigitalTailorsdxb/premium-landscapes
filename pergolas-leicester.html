Pergola & Garden Structure Types We Build

Traditional Pressure-Treated Timber Pergolas

The most popular and best-value option — softwood beams and posts in tanalised brown or grey, simple cross-beam roof structure perfect for climbing plants like wisteria, jasmine or clematis. Built in any size from 2x2m to 6x4m.

Solid Oak Pergolas

A premium upgrade using green oak posts and beams that silver beautifully over 2-3 years. Heavier, more sculptural and exceptionally long-lasting — a true investment piece for a high-end Leicester garden. Often paired with porcelain paving and natural stone walls.

Aluminium & Louvered-Roof Pergolas

Modern aluminium-frame pergolas with electric louvered roofs that open, close and angle for any weather — true year-round outdoor living. Optional integrated LED lighting, heaters and side screens turn the structure into a fully-enclosed garden room.

Gazebos & Octagonal Structures

Free-standing gazebos with full roofs for shelter from rain and full shade in summer. Ideal as a focal point at the end of a garden, beside a pond or as a hot tub enclosure.

Arbours, Arches & Seating Structures

Smaller architectural touches — entrance arches over a pathway, arbours with built-in benches, planted dividers between garden zones. Made to measure to suit your garden style.

Why Build On-Site Rather Than Buy a Kit?

Off-the-shelf pergola kits from DIY stores look like off-the-shelf pergola kits. The dimensions never quite fit your space, the timber is often lower-grade, and the fixings rarely match the load requirements of a permanent garden structure. The result is a pergola that looks generic and starts wobbling within 3 to 4 years.

Every pergola we build is dimensioned to your garden — exact post spacing to clear existing features, beam heights to clear doors and windows, depths sized to your patio, and finishing details (decorative end cuts, post caps, lighting recesses) that no kit can match.

Structurally, our pergolas are bolted onto galvanised post anchors set into concrete footings, with stainless steel through-bolts at every beam junction. Built to last 20+ years and certainly capable of supporting heavy climbing plants, integrated lighting and even retractable shade systems.

Where to Position a Pergola in Your Leicester Garden

The best pergola positions take advantage of sun direction, view and the rest of the garden layout. Most Leicester gardens benefit from a pergola placed at the end of an existing patio (creating a defined dining or seating zone), or as a stand-alone destination at the end of a garden path with a planted route leading to it.

South or west-facing positions provide afternoon sun and golden-hour shade. North-facing pergolas work well as planted screens or seating bowers but receive less direct sun. We always consider sightlines from your house, prevailing wind direction and the views you want to frame (or block) before finalising design.

Integration with Other Garden Elements

A pergola rarely sits in isolation — the best ones are designed alongside the patio, lighting, planting and even the fencing they sit against. When we design a pergola we consider:

  • Patio dimensions and material — square pergolas sit best over square paving zones
  • Lighting — LED uplighters on posts and downlighters in beams transform evening use
  • Climbing plants — wire systems and planting pits for wisteria, roses, grapevines
  • Heating — outdoor electric heaters and infrared panels for shoulder-season use
  • Privacy — slatted or planted side screens for boundary positions
  • Hot tub enclosure — engineered for the moisture and structural load required
