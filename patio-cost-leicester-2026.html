How Much Does a Patio Cost in Leicester in 2026?

By Lewis Reid · 6 years landscaping experience | 23 June 2026 | Patio Guide

Patio prices vary more than most people expect — not because contractors make up numbers, but because the variables are genuinely significant. Material choice, garden size, sub-base condition, access, waste disposal, and ground conditions all affect the final cost. In Leicester, clay soil adds a layer of complexity that doesn't apply in every part of the UK.

This guide breaks down what a patio actually costs in Leicester in 2026, why certain jobs cost more than others, and what to watch out for when comparing quotes.

Price Per m² by Material

These are installed prices — materials, labour, sub-base, and jointing included. They assume standard access and reasonable ground conditions.

Porcelain paving: £90–£140/m²

The most popular choice in Leicester right now. Low maintenance, frost-resistant, and available in a wide range of formats. Larger format tiles (900×600, 1200×600) cost more to lay due to the cutting precision required.

Indian sandstone: £70–£100/m²

A proven performer in UK gardens. Warmer tones than porcelain. Needs sealing annually in Leicester's climate to prevent moss and staining. Good value for larger patios.

Granite: £100–£150/m²

The most durable natural stone option. Frost-proof, low maintenance when flamed, long lifespan. Higher material cost but lower lifetime cost than softer stones.

Limestone: £75–£120/m²

Elegant look that suits period Leicester properties. More porous than granite — sealing is non-negotiable in the UK climate. Jura limestone is the premium option; Indian limestone is budget-friendly.

Block paving (for patios): £70–£110/m²

Increasingly popular for garden patio areas as well as driveways. Permeable options available that resolve SUDS drainage requirements without planning consent.

Concrete flags: £45–£70/m²

Functional and affordable. Not the most attractive option but performs well under heavy use. Often used in side passages or utility areas.

What Affects the Total Cost

Size: Most patio contractors quote per m² but have a minimum job cost — typically £1,500–£2,000. Small patios (under 20m²) often cost more per m² than larger ones because set-up costs are fixed.

Sub-base condition: If we're digging out an existing concrete base or dealing with buried rubble (common in Leicester Victorian terraces), disposal costs are higher. Skips in LE postcodes currently run £200–£350 depending on size and location.

Leicester clay soil: Clay subsoils require deeper excavation — minimum 200mm — and more hardcore to create a stable base. Sand over clay without adequate hardcore results in a patio that sinks at the edges within 3–5 years. Contractors who quote cheap on clay-heavy sites are usually skimping here.

Access: Restricted access (narrow side passage, terraced property with no rear access) means materials are wheelbarrowed through the house or over the fence — this adds half a day's labour to most jobs.

Drainage: A patio without a proper drainage fall will pond water. We install a minimum 1:80 fall away from the house on all our patios. Adding a linear drain at the patio edge for high-volume drainage adds £150–£400 depending on length.

Pattern and cuts: Straight, running-bond layouts are fastest to lay. Intricate patterns (herringbone, feature circles, alternating formats) require more cutting and more time — expect 10–20% on top of a straight-lay price.

How to Compare Quotes Without Being Caught Out

Three things to check when quotes come in significantly below others:

Sub-base specification: Ask what depth of MOT Type 1 hardcore they're installing and how it's being compacted. The right answer for Leicester clay is 100–150mm compacted hardcore minimum, laid in layers with a vibrating plate. "We'll lay it on sharp sand" is a red flag.

Waste disposal: Ask if skip hire or waste removal is included. Some contractors quote labour and materials only — you then discover the skip is extra.

Mortar bed vs spot bedding: Porcelain and natural stone should be laid on a full mortar bed. Spot bedding (four corner blobs of adhesive) cuts 20–30% off laying time and creates hollow spots that crack the stone within a few years. Ask which method they use.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a 40m² patio cost in Leicester?

A 40m² patio in Indian sandstone typically costs £3,000–£4,200 installed. In porcelain, expect £3,800–£5,600. The range reflects material grade, sub-base requirements, and site access. We provide fixed-price quotes that don't change once agreed.

Why are some patio quotes so much cheaper than others?

A significantly lower quote often means shallow sub-base (sinks within 5 years on Leicester clay), spot bedding instead of a full mortar bed, or skip hire added as an extra at the end.

Does Leicester clay soil make patios more expensive?

It adds cost at the sub-base stage — deeper excavation and more hardcore to get a stable bed. A correctly built patio on clay costs 10–15% more than one on stable sandy ground. It's worth it — a correctly built base lasts 20+ years.

Is porcelain worth the extra cost over sandstone?

For most Leicester gardens, yes. Porcelain doesn't need sealing, is frost-proof, and doesn't stain from BBQ grease or red wine. Sandstone needs annual sealing and will mark if neglected. Over 10 years, the maintenance cost gap closes significantly.

How long does a patio installation take in Leicester?

A 40m² patio takes 2–3 days for an experienced crew — one day for dig-out and sub-base, one to two days for laying and jointing. Larger patios with complex patterns take 4–5 days.
