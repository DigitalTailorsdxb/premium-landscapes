Planning Permission & Narborough

Local Planning Authority: Blaby District Council

Responsible for planning decisions in Narborough and the surrounding LE19 area.

Blaby District Council is the local planning authority for Narborough. Most domestic patio, decking and garden work is permitted development. Any driveway replacing a grass front garden requires a SuDS-compliant permeable surface or a planning application — block paving with permeable jointing or resin bound gravel both comply. We advise on the correct surface specification for each project.

What typically does not need planning permission in Narborough

  • Rear garden patio and hard landscaping at ground level
  • Artificial grass in rear or front gardens (replacing natural grass)
  • Composite or timber decking up to 30cm above ground level and not more than 50% of the rear garden
  • Garden lighting using low-voltage (12V) transformers
  • Rear garden fencing up to 2 metres high

What may require planning consent in Narborough

  • Decking over 30cm high or covering more than 50% of the rear garden
  • Front garden hard-surfacing over 5m² replacing grass (must use permeable surface or obtain permission)
  • Work in a designated conservation area or on a listed building
  • Large outbuildings or pergolas exceeding the permitted development size limits

Planning rules can change and are subject to local variations. We provide planning guidance at no charge during our survey visit — if in doubt, always verify with Blaby District Council directly before proceeding.
