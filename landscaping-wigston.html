Planning Permission & Wigston

Local Planning Authority: Oadby & Wigston Borough Council

Responsible for planning decisions in Wigston and the surrounding LE18 area.

Oadby & Wigston Borough Council is the local planning authority. South Wigston has a Conservation Area covering the historic textile workers' housing on Bull Head Street and surrounds — permitted development restrictions apply to properties within it. Standard residential patio, decking and artificial grass installations outside the conservation area are generally permitted development.

What typically does not need planning permission in Wigston

  • Rear garden patio and hard landscaping at ground level
  • Artificial grass in rear or front gardens (replacing natural grass)
  • Composite or timber decking up to 30cm above ground level and not more than 50% of the rear garden
  • Garden lighting using low-voltage (12V) transformers
  • Rear garden fencing up to 2 metres high

What may require planning consent in Wigston

  • Decking over 30cm high or covering more than 50% of the rear garden
  • Front garden hard-surfacing over 5m² replacing grass (must use permeable surface or obtain permission)
  • Work in a designated conservation area or on a listed building
  • Large outbuildings or pergolas exceeding the permitted development size limits

Planning rules can change and are subject to local variations. We provide planning guidance at no charge during our survey visit — if in doubt, always verify with Oadby & Wigston Borough Council directly before proceeding.
