Who We Are

Premium Landscapes is an established landscaping company based at 44 Barwell Road, Kirby Muxloe, on the western edge of Leicester. We are a small, hands-on team of landscapers, designers and groundwork specialists working exclusively across Leicester and Leicestershire.

We are not a national franchise, a lead-generation site or a middleman. Every project we quote is one we install ourselves, with our own team. That is why we can give fixed prices, guarantee workmanship and explain exactly what is and is not included in your quote — there is no second contractor in the chain.

The business was built around one idea: the quote and design process for landscaping is broken. Homeowners wait days or weeks for a quote, get pressured into decisions, and rarely see what their finished garden will look like before they commit. So we built a free online tool that solves all three problems in a single visit.

Our Quote & AI Design System

Our free instant quote and AI design tool was built so Leicester homeowners can plan a garden project on their own terms, without booking a salesperson before they know what they want.

Here is how it works:

  • Step 1 — Tell us about your project. A short, conversational form asks what you want (patio, lawn, full makeover), the rough size, your budget range and your postcode. There are two modes: select individual products, or describe a full garden makeover in your own words.
  • Step 2 — Get an instant itemised quote. Our pricing engine returns a real cost range within minutes, broken down by material and labour, using current Leicester prices.
  • Step 3 — Get a free AI garden design (optional). Upload a photo of your existing garden and the AI generates a photorealistic visualisation of the finished design. Delivered to your email within 90 seconds.
  • Step 4 — Decide what happens next. The quote and design are yours to keep, with no obligation. If you would like to proceed, we book a free site visit to confirm details and lock in a fixed price.

There is no charge for any part of this, no requirement to book a sales call, and no obligation at any stage.
