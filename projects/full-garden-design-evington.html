The Finished Garden

A contemporary full garden transformation in Evington, Leicester.

Project Overview

This Evington homeowner had recently completed a rear extension and wanted the garden to match the quality and finish of their newly renovated home. The existing garden was a blank canvas — stripped back to bare earth by the builders — giving us the rare opportunity to design from scratch.

The brief centred on creating a garden that felt like a true extension of the interior: clean lines, quality materials, and a design that could be enjoyed from inside the house as well as out. The homeowner also wanted low maintenance at the forefront of every decision.

We used our AI design tool to generate three concept directions based on their mood board references, which the customer then refined into a final brief. The result is a contemporary, well-structured garden with clear zones — dining, lawn, and planting — that flows beautifully from the new bifold doors.

Materials Used

600×900mm Porcelain Paving 35mm Luxury Artificial Turf Composite Raised Beds Hardwood Sleeper Edging LED Uplighting System Painted Featherboard Fencing Ornamental Planting Scheme

Scope of Work

  • Full design consultation and AI-powered visualisation
  • Complete clearance of existing garden
  • New close-board fencing to all three boundaries
  • 50m² porcelain patio with step and raised border
  • 25m² artificial grass lawn with curved edging
  • Two hardwood sleeper raised beds with planting
  • 8-light LED garden lighting system
  • Full ornamental planting scheme

How We Did It

A step-by-step look at the transformation process.

1

Design Consultation

Three AI-generated concept directions were presented based on the customer's brief. The contemporary minimal scheme was selected and developed into full working drawings.

2

Site Clearance & Prep

The site was cleared, levelled and drainage assessed. New slatted fencing and decorative screen panels were installed to all boundaries before any hard landscaping began.

3

Water Feature & Features

The bespoke water feature was constructed with stone cladding, a stainless blade weir and rendered plinth. Decorative rose screen panels were installed as the backdrop focal point.

4

Decking & Patio

The composite decking zone was installed adjacent to the house, creating a raised dining terrace. Beyond it, the granite sett patio with circular seating plinth was laid and pointed.

5

Lawn & Lighting

Premium artificial grass was laid to the circular lawn zone with shaped porcelain edging. The 8-light LED system was installed into the lawn borders and commissioned.

6

Planting & Handover

The ornamental planting scheme was installed across the raised beds and borders. The garden was cleaned, photographed and handed over with all documentation.

The Results

The Evington project is one of our most photographed — and it's easy to see why. The combination of large-format porcelain, curved artificial lawn and sleeper-edged raised beds creates a garden with genuine visual depth and interest from every angle.

The bespoke water feature with stainless blade weir provides a focal point that works in all seasons, while the 8-light LED system means the garden reads equally well at dusk. The result is a space that works as well experienced outside as it does viewed through the new bi-fold doors.

Completed in 10 days. Photographed on handover day.
