Why Choose Natural Turf Over Artificial Grass?

Both have their place — but natural turf is the right choice when you want a genuine lawn experience. It is cooler underfoot in summer (artificial can get very hot), supports wildlife and pollinators, absorbs rainwater rather than shedding it, smells like a real garden, and costs around half the price of artificial grass to install. With modern low-maintenance grass varieties, a natural lawn no longer requires the hours of upkeep it once did.

Natural turf is especially well-suited to Leicester gardens with good drainage, partial shade tolerance and families who enjoy gardening or have wildlife-friendly aspirations. Artificial grass remains the better choice for very shady gardens, gardens with multiple dogs, or homeowners who want zero maintenance — see our artificial grass page for that option.

What Premium Turfing Includes

Every Premium Landscapes turfing installation includes the full preparation process — because a beautiful lawn starts with what is underneath. Skipping preparation steps is the most common cause of failed lawns:

  • Removal of existing lawn, weeds, debris and old turf
  • Rotovating to break up compacted soil and improve aeration
  • Application of fresh screened topsoil where needed (minimum 50mm)
  • Pre-turf fertiliser worked into the surface for strong root establishment
  • Levelling and raking to a fine tilth — no bumps, dips or stones
  • Premium rolled turf delivered fresh from UK farm (laid within 24 hours of delivery)
  • Turf laid with tight butted joints and brick-bond pattern, edges trimmed neatly
  • Initial heavy watering and rolling for soil contact
  • Written aftercare guide and watering schedule provided

When to Lay Turf in Leicester

Turf can be laid at any time of year except in heavy frost or during prolonged drought. The two ideal periods in Leicester are mid-September to late November (warm soil + autumn rain establishes roots quickly) and late February through to early May. Summer turfing is fine but requires more diligent daily watering for the first 3 to 4 weeks.

Fresh turf must not be walked on for 2 to 3 weeks while roots establish. We provide stepping boards if you must cross the lawn during this period, and full written aftercare guidance.

Aftercare & First Mow Guidance

Watering

Heavy watering daily for the first 14 days, then taper to every other day. The soil beneath the turf must stay moist down to root depth — a quick surface sprinkle is not enough. Early morning or evening watering only, never midday sun.

First Mow

Wait until the grass reaches 5-6cm high — typically 3 to 4 weeks after laying — then cut on the highest setting only, removing no more than one third of the blade. Gradually lower the cutting height over subsequent mows.

Long-Term Care

Twice-yearly feeds (spring and autumn), aeration every 2 to 3 years and annual scarifying will keep a Leicester lawn in excellent condition for decades.
